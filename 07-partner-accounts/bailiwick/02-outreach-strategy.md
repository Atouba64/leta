# Outreach Strategy: Bailiwick

## 1. Value Proposition & Key Arguments
When speaking with Bailiwick, we must emphasize these core arguments:
* **Project-Ready Crews:** Whether it's one site or fifty sites across Georgia, Leta can allocate the right number of low-voltage techs to meet your deployment schedule.
* **Real-time Deliverables:** Your PMs in Minnesota don't need to chase techs in Georgia for site photos. Our app forces photo uploads of mounted APs and cleaned-up MDF rooms before the job is marked complete.

## 2. Target Personas
To break into Bailiwick, we need to target the following roles:
* Vendor Manager
* Deployment Project Manager
* Field Operations Director

## 3. Contact Avenues
* Bailiwick Vendor Portal (they are actively always recruiting local installation partners).
* Targeting Rollout Project Managers and Vendor Managers on LinkedIn.
* Calling their vendor management department directly.

## 4. The "Leta Difference" Pitch for Bailiwick
"You are experts at managing national and global IT portfolios. We are the experts at executing the physical layer in Georgia. By partnering with Leta, you bypass the friction of managing disparate 1099s and gain an invisible, highly accountable field force that makes your SLA compliance effortless."
