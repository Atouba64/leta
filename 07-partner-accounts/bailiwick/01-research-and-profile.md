# Partner Profile: Bailiwick

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Chaska, MN
* **Website:** https://www.bailiwick.com
* **Core Focus:** IT infrastructure, cabling, and WLAN deployments for Fortune 500 retail/logistics.

## 2. Mode of Operation
Bailiwick is famous for executing massive, multi-site IT deployments (e.g., upgrading Wi-Fi in 1,000 retail stores). They act as the overarching project manager and rely heavily on a network of vetted sub-contractors and local field service companies to do the physical pulling of cable and mounting of APs (Access Points).

## 3. How Leta Serves Them
Bailiwick requires teams that understand low-voltage cabling, networking, and commercial environments. Leta can tag and filter our bench for "Layer 1/Cabling" techs, offering Bailiwick a turnkey crew for their Georgia rollouts.

## 4. Operational Alignment
* **Dispatch Model:** Leta integrates as a primary fulfillment arm for their Georgia-based tickets.
* **Platform Fit:** They can utilize the Leta Partner Portal to inject work orders, or Leta can consume tickets from their existing portal, keeping status updated in real-time.
