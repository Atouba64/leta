# Partner Profile: Leapfrog IT Services

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Atlanta, GA
* **Website:** https://leapfrogservices.com
* **Core Focus:** Premium Managed IT Services and cybersecurity for mid-to-large enterprises.

## 2. Mode of Operation
Leapfrog is one of the most prominent and established MSPs headquartered in Atlanta. They provide premium helpdesk, cloud, and security services. While they have their own W2 field engineers for immediate Metro Atlanta needs, sending premium engineers to branch offices in Savannah, Augusta, or Columbus is a massive drain on their resources and profitability.

## 3. How Leta Serves Them
Leta serves as Leapfrog’s "Statewide Smart Hands." We allow their Level 2 and Level 3 engineers to stay at the NOC in Atlanta while our vetted techs drive to the client sites across the state of Georgia to rack switches, swap firewalls, and troubleshoot physical connectivity.

## 4. Operational Alignment
* **Dispatch Model:** Leta integrates as a primary fulfillment arm for their Georgia-based tickets.
* **Platform Fit:** They can utilize the Leta Partner Portal to inject work orders, or Leta can consume tickets from their existing portal, keeping status updated in real-time.
