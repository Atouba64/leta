# Outreach Strategy: Leapfrog IT Services

## 1. Value Proposition & Key Arguments
When speaking with Leapfrog IT Services, we must emphasize these core arguments:
* **Protect Your Premium Talent:** Stop paying a Level 3 engineer to sit in traffic on I-75 to swap a router in Macon. Send a Leta tech for the physical swap while your engineer configures it remotely.
* **Hometown Partnership:** We are both Georgia businesses. We understand the local geography, traffic patterns, and culture better than a national dispatch center.
* **White-Label Professionalism:** Leapfrog clients expect a premium experience. Leta techs are highly vetted and act as a seamless, professional extension of the Leapfrog brand on-site.

## 2. Target Personas
To break into Leapfrog IT Services, we need to target the following roles:
* Director of Operations
* Field Services Manager
* VP of Service Delivery

## 3. Contact Avenues
* Direct local outreach to their Atlanta headquarters.
* Targeting their Director of Field Services or VP of Operations on LinkedIn.
* Networking at local Atlanta tech associations (TAG - Technology Association of Georgia).

## 4. The "Leta Difference" Pitch for Leapfrog IT Services
"As an MSP scaling across Georgia, managing physical dispatches is your biggest margin-killer. By partnering with Leta, you get elastic, on-demand smart hands across the entire state. You keep your top engineers remote and highly utilized, while our vetted local techs handle the physical execution with guaranteed SLAs."
