# Field Engineer Policy
Do's and Don'ts for Client Site Visits

## ✅ Do's for Field Engineers

- **Arrive on time** – Punctuality builds trust. Inform the client if there is any delay.
- **Dress professionally** – Wear company uniform or neat attire with visible ID badge.
- **Introduce yourself properly** – State your name, company, and purpose of visit.
- **Follow site entry protocols** – Sign in, adhere to visitor policies, and respect security checks.
- **Communicate clearly** – Explain the work you’ll be doing and approximate timelines.
- **Respect client’s space** – Keep work areas tidy and avoid unnecessary disruptions.
- **Document work** – Take notes, photos (if allowed), and update service logs.
- **Follow safety guidelines** – Use PPE if required and comply with health & safety regulations.
- **Be courteous & professional** – Use polite language, listen carefully, and acknowledge client concerns.
- **Report issues immediately** – Escalate any blockers, risks, or unexpected problems to your manager.
- **Ensure client sign-off** – Before leaving, confirm completion with the client and get acknowledgment.
- **Leave no trace** – Clean up the workspace and return things as they were.

## ❌ Don'ts for Field Engineers

- **Don’t arrive unprepared** – Always review the job details before visiting.
- **Don’t misrepresent your role** – Never claim authority you don’t have.
- **Don’t use client’s resources without permission** – (Wi-Fi, power outlets, office equipment, etc.)
- **Don’t share confidential information** – Avoid discussing other clients or internal company matters.
- **Don’t ignore safety rules** – Cutting corners can damage trust and cause hazards.
- **Don’t leave tasks incomplete** – Without proper documentation or escalation.
- **Don’t argue with clients** – If disagreements arise, escalate politely instead of debating.
- **Don’t use personal devices for work** – Unless explicitly approved (data security risk).
- **Don’t loiter or overstay** – Once the job is done and signed off, exit professionally.
- **Don’t post about the visit on social media** – Especially with client details or photos.
