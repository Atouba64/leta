# Partner Profile: Barrister Global Services Network

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Hammond, LA (with a large national and international footprint)
* **Phone:** 1-800-213-9008
* **Website:** barrister.com
* **Core Focus:** National IT field service broker / fulfillment network covering break/fix, retail, deployments, cabling, and POS systems.

## 2. What They Do (Mode of Operation)
Barrister operates as a massive legacy aggregator. They claim a network of 13,000–15,000+ technicians across the US and Canada. Their model relies heavily on independent 1099 contractors, using BAM/TECHWA-style portals. Their dispatch culture is heavily reliant on phone calls and emails to manage tech check-ins and deliverables.

## 3. How Leta Solves Their Exact Pain Points
Barrister's biggest systemic friction is the "telephone game" of dispatch—dispatchers are buried in "where's the tech?" calls and 50-person Teams bridges for support. Furthermore, they struggle with rural and secondary market coverage in states like Georgia.
*   **The Leta Solution:** Leta acts as an overflow and specialist routing partner. We provide our API-driven, offline-first application that handles timeline visibility and Ticket-scoped messaging. We eliminate their dispatch overhead for Georgia by providing a single, highly accountable execution arm with real-time ETA tracking and guaranteed deliverables.

## 4. Operational Alignment
*   **Technical Integration:** We offer them a "Georgia Wedge Pilot." They route their hard-to-fill or rural Georgia tickets to Leta. They keep the enterprise customer of record; we act as the invisible white-labeled field force.
