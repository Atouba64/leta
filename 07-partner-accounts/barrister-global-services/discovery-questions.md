# Discovery questions — Barrister

Use after rapport on first or second call. **Listen 70% / talk 30%.**

## Volume & geography

1. How many **onsite tickets per month** touch **Georgia** today?  
2. Which **metros or counties** are hardest to fill within SLA?  
3. Any **rural or long-travel** jobs you decline or miss SLA on?

## Dispatch pain

4. How does dispatch know a tech is **en route / on site** today?  
5. How many **inbound status calls** per day from upstream customers or internal account teams?  
6. What breaks most often — **assignment**, **travel**, **scope**, or **close-out documentation**?

## Economics

7. Typical **rate bands** for GA break/fix vs multi-hour vs specialist (Cradlepoint, POS, etc.)?  
8. Do you use **flat** vs **hourly** more often?  
9. What **mobilization / travel** rules do techs expect?

## Integration & process

10. Can partners pass a **stable partner WO #** on every ticket?  
11. **POC-only** sites — how often is that required?  
12. Who joins **remote support** today — Teams bridge, vendor engineer, both?

## Decision process

13. What does a **new Georgia fulfillment partner** approval path look like?  
14. Insurance / COI requirements for subs?  
15. Earliest date a **pilot** could start if we align this month?

## Close

16. “If we hit **X KPI** on 10 tickets, what happens next — expand counties, rate card, or formal vendor ID?”
