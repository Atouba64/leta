# Objections & responses — Barrister conversations

## “We already have 13,000 techs.”

> “That’s exactly why we’re not selling you more techs nationally — we’re offering **Georgia density and visibility** when the national pool is thin or dispatch is on the phone chasing status. Think **overflow and specialist routing**, not replacement.”

## “We have our own portal.”

> “Keep it. Leta sits **alongside** for the pilot — your WO numbers, our timeline and ticket thread. If it reduces calls to your desk, we integrate deeper later.”

## “We don’t know you.”

> “Fair. That’s why we propose **5–10 tickets**, weekly KPI report, and references from your dispatch lead — not a national contract on day one.”

## “Our techs won’t use another app.”

> “Our techs are **1099 Georgia crew** on Leta — not your internal roster. You’re not retraining 13k people; you’re tapping a **local bench** we recruit and vet.”

## “Rate will be too high.”

> “We can route **economy break-fix** or **specialist** WOs — you tag the skill level. Rate is **locked at accept**, not renegotiated on site. Happy to compare one SKU side by side.”

## “Legal / insurance won’t allow it.”

> “Understood. What does your **vendor onboarding packet** require? We carry GL path for techs and can meet COI template — walk me through your checklist.”

## “Send an email.”

> “I will today. Can we also book **15 minutes** Thursday or Friday so I answer questions live? Who else should join — dispatch or vendor management?”

## “Call corporate / someone else.”

> “Thank you — who’s the right name and number? I’ll mention you connected us. Can I use your name as the intro?”

## “Not interested.”

> “Appreciate the honesty. When do you **re-evaluate Southeast subs**? Can I check back in one quarter?”
