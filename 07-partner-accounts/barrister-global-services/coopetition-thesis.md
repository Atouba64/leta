# Coopetition thesis — Barrister × Leta

## One paragraph

Barrister and Leta are **not mutually exclusive**. Barrister (and firms like it) aggregate **enterprise demand** and a **national contractor pool**; Leta builds **modern fulfillment mechanics** in **Georgia** — visibility, ticket-level comms, skills-based bench, faster close-out. When Barrister’s bottleneck is **capacity or transparency in specific markets**, routing overflow to Leta **protects their SLA** and **reduces dispatcher load** without Barrister losing the customer narrative.

## Three messages for Barrister-facing calls

1. **“We extend your network — we don’t replace your brand.”**  
   End customer still sees Barrister (or their Tier-2). Leta is smart hands + platform.

2. **“We fix the call-center problem around field status.”**  
   Partner portal + logged comms = fewer 1-800 loops.

3. **“Georgia first — prove it where you feel pain.”**  
   Low-risk pilot; measurable KPIs; expand only if data supports it.

## What Barrister gets

| Benefit | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|
| SLA protection in thin markets | More accepted WOs in GA radii |
| Fewer dispatcher minutes | Status in portal vs phone |
| Specialist tickets done right | Skill tags on WO + highlighted tech profiles |
| Audit trail for upstream customer | Photos, sign-off, partner WO # preserved |
| Optional economy tier | Cost-effective break-fix when margin matters |

## What Leta gets

| Benefit | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|
| Volume | Real WOs with real SLAs |
| Credibility | “Barrister-class partner” reference (if pilot succeeds) |
| Product hardening | GUS patterns, POC rules, rate cards |

## Red lines (never say on first call)

- Attacking Barrister tech pay publicly  
- “Your techs hate you”  
- Promising national scale week one  
- Claiming legal/regulatory findings as facts  

## Red lines (internal only — product ethics)

Leta must **not** replicate: post-accept rate slashes, unpaid on-site wait for remote, travel without mobilization. See forensics for **what not to build**.
