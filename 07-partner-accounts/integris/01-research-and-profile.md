# Partner Profile: Integris (Atlanta Hub)

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Atlanta, GA (Major Regional Hub, formerly ProviDyn)
* **Website:** https://integrisit.com
* **Core Focus:** National MSP delivering localized, high-touch IT support.

## 2. Mode of Operation
Integris is a massive national MSP that grew by acquiring elite local MSPs (including Atlanta-based ProviDyn). They operate on a "pod" model, meaning their Atlanta office acts as a massive regional hub servicing hundreds of businesses across Georgia. They handle everything from daily helpdesk to complex network deployments.

## 3. How Leta Serves Them
As Integris scales their Georgia client base, their local dispatch team can get overwhelmed by spikes in on-site tickets. Leta acts as their elastic field bench. If their internal W2 team is fully booked, they route the overflow to Leta, ensuring their local SLAs never slip.

## 4. Operational Alignment
* **Dispatch Model:** Leta integrates as a primary fulfillment arm for their Georgia-based tickets.
* **Platform Fit:** They can utilize the Leta Partner Portal to inject work orders, or Leta can consume tickets from their existing portal, keeping status updated in real-time.
