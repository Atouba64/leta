# Partner Profile: Essintial Enterprise Solutions

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Mechanicsburg, PA
* **Website:** https://www.essintial.com
* **Core Focus:** Enterprise IT field services and supply chain logistics.

## 2. Mode of Operation
Essintial combines logistics (storing hardware parts in forward-stocking locations) with field services. If a server fails, they coordinate getting the replacement part from a local depot to the site, along with a tech to install it. They use a network of "Independent Service Providers" (ISPs).

## 3. How Leta Serves Them
Leta is the perfect ISP for Essintial in Georgia. We can coordinate our techs to pick up hardware from their Atlanta-area forward stocking locations and deploy to the client site, providing the "hands" to their "parts".

## 4. Operational Alignment
* **Dispatch Model:** Leta integrates as a primary fulfillment arm for their Georgia-based tickets.
* **Platform Fit:** They can utilize the Leta Partner Portal to inject work orders, or Leta can consume tickets from their existing portal, keeping status updated in real-time.
