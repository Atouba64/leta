# Step-by-Step Execution Guide: Closing Kinettix

This document provides a highly detailed, day-by-day, self-explanatory guide on exactly how to pursue and close Kinettix as a Leta partner. 

*If you are new to this process, please read the `00-LETA-PARTNER-ACQUISITION-PLAYBOOK.md` in the parent directory first.*

---

## Phase 1: The Initial Approach (Days 1-5)

### Day 1: Target Identification
1. **Action:** Open LinkedIn and type "Kinettix" into the search bar.
2. **Action:** Filter the results by "People".
3. **Action:** Search for the specific job titles listed in `02-outreach-strategy.md` (e.g., "Vendor Manager", "Field Operations Director", "Service Delivery").
4. **Goal:** Identify 2 to 3 key decision-makers. Save their profile URLs to your CRM or spreadsheet.

### Day 2: The Soft Touch (Establishing Social Proof)
1. **Action:** Send a LinkedIn Connection Request to the 2-3 targets you identified.
2. **Crucial Rule:** **DO NOT PITCH IN THE CONNECTION REQUEST.** 
3. **Action:** Use the exact short, polite script provided in `03-call-scripts-and-emails.md`. The only goal today is to get them to click "Accept."

### Day 3: The Direct Email Pitch
1. **Condition A:** If they accepted your LinkedIn request, send them a direct LinkedIn message using the "Cold Email" template from `03-call-scripts-and-emails.md`.
2. **Condition B:** If they did not accept, use an email discovery tool (like Hunter.io, Apollo.io, or RocketReach) to find their corporate email address.
3. **Action:** Send the highly targeted "Georgia Coverage/Smart Hands" email. Ensure the subject line is exactly as written in the script.

### Day 5: The Follow-Up & Gatekeeper Navigation (Cold Call)
1. **Action:** Call the corporate headquarters of Kinettix or the direct line of your target.
2. **If you hit a Gatekeeper (Receptionist or Assistant):**
   * Do not pitch the gatekeeper. 
   * Say exactly: *"Hi, I'm trying to reach the person who manages your third-party IT field vendors in the Southeast. Who would be the best person to speak with regarding Georgia dispatch?"*
3. **If you get the Target on the phone:**
   * Use the "Discovery Call Script" from `03-call-scripts-and-emails.md`. 
   * **Rule:** Keep it under 2 minutes. Talk 20% of the time, let them talk 80% of the time. Focus entirely on *their* pain points (missed SLAs, lack of visibility, expensive W2 travel).

---

## Phase 2: The "Pilot" Proposition (Days 6-14)

Enterprise clients will rarely hand over their entire state volume on Day 1. You must step them into the relationship.

### Day 6-10: Securing the Pilot
* **Action:** When you get them on a meeting, propose a risk-free trial.
* **The Script:** *"We don't expect you to move all your Georgia tickets to us immediately. Send us 3 of your hardest-to-staff tickets in Georgia this month (e.g., a rural location like Valdosta, or a last-minute emergency in Atlanta). Let us prove our SLA and show you how our portal visibility works on those 3 tickets."*

### Day 11-14: Navigating the Vendor Portal
* Large companies like Kinettix have strict compliance. They will require you to register in their formal Vendor Management System (VMS) or procurement portal.
* **Action:** Ask them directly: *"Could you send me the link to your vendor onboarding packet?"*
* **Execution:** 
  1. Fill out their onboarding packet.
  2. Upload Leta's COI (Certificate of Insurance) and W-9.
  3. Review and sign their MSA (Master Service Agreement). *Note: Ensure you are comfortable with their payment terms (usually Net-30 or Net-45).*

---

## Phase 3: Operational Integration (Post-Signing)

### Step 1: Portal Training & Handoff
1. **Action:** Once approved as a vendor, invite their dispatch team to the Leta Partner Portal (`leta.repair/partners/portal.html`).
2. **Action:** Host a 15-minute Zoom call to show their dispatchers how to submit a ticket to Leta and how to view the "Live Thread".

### Step 2: flawless Execution of the First Dispatch
1. **Action:** When their first ticket comes in, manually monitor it like a hawk.
2. **Action:** Ensure the assigned Leta tech checks in on time, uploads the required crisp photos, and gets the site manager's signature.
3. **Action:** Email your primary contact at Kinettix immediately after the first ticket closes: 
   * *"Hi [Name], our first ticket with you is complete. SLAs were met. The audit photos and manager signatures are attached in the portal for your review."* 
   * (Doing this builds massive trust and guarantees they will send you more volume).
