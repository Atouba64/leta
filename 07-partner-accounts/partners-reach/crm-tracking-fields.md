## Stage definitions

1. **Research** — account profile written  
2. **Outreach** — first call attempted  
3. **Conversation** — meeting or demo scheduled  
4. **Pilot scoping** — counties, rates, ticket format  
5. **Pilot live** — WOs flowing  
6. **Production** — MSA + steady volume
