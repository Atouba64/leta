# Follow-up cadence — partner outreach

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **0** | First call + same-day email (use [`email-templates.md`](./email-templates.md)) |
| **1** | LinkedIn connect with note: “Thanks for the conversation on GA fulfillment.” |
| **2** | If no reply: one text to business line only if they agreed on call |
| **5** | Email bump |
| **10** | Call #2 — shorter; ask for intro to vendor management / ops lead |
| **14** | Offer concrete pilot doc ([`../barrister-global-services/georgia-wedge-pilot.md`](../barrister-global-services/georgia-wedge-pilot.md)) |
| **21** | “Close the loop” email — pause or quarterly check-in |
| **30** | Add to nurture list; revisit when GA coverage expands |

## If they say “send info”

Send within **4 business hours**:

1. Link: [leta.repair/partners/workflow.html](https://leta.repair/partners/workflow.html)  
2. One-pager: pilot scope + KPIs (partner-specific folder)  
3. Propose **two times** for demo  

## If they say “not now”

Ask: **“When do you re-bid Southeast coverage?”** and **“Who should I stay in touch with?”**

Log in [`crm-tracking-fields.md`](./crm-tracking-fields.md).
