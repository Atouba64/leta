# Partner outreach strategy — Leta

## Ideal partner profile (ICP)

| Trait | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Holds **SLA** to an enterprise or OEM | They feel pain when bench is thin |
| **Georgia** (or Southeast) volume they cannot always fill | Our launch wedge |
| Uses **phone/email dispatch** today | Clear before/after with portal + thread |
| Willing to pass **partner WO #** on every ticket | GUS / audit requirement |

**Barrister** fits: national broker, 13k+ tech claim, known GA retail/IT volume, legacy portal + phone culture.

## Positioning (one sentence)

**Leta is the modern fulfillment layer for onsite IT in Georgia — you keep the customer; we add bench, live status, and ticket-scoped comms so your desk stops being a call center.**

## Channels (priority order)

1. **Warm intro** — founder network, former Barrister/Qmatic contacts, LinkedIn mutuals  
2. **Direct phone** — ops/vendor/partner desk (e.g. Enver)  
3. **Email** — `partners@leta.repair` + personalized subject  
4. **LinkedIn** — connect after call, not before (higher accept rate post-conversation)  
5. **Events** — regional MSP meetups, retail IT forums (low cost)  

## Georgia-first wedge (why now)

- We are **building density** in GA metros + rural corridors (not “national someday”).  
- Pilot = **5–10 WOs** with KPI: fewer status calls, on-time arrival, audit trail.  
- Low risk for them: **overflow / gap-fill**, not rip-and-replace.

## Coopetition frame (critical for Barrister-class)

| Say | Avoid |
|-----|--------|
| “Overflow bench when your network is thin in GA” | “We’re better than Barrister” |
| “Visibility layer your dispatchers can trust” | “Your techs hate you” (even if true) |
| “Pilot alongside existing process” | “Replace your portal tomorrow” |

## Win criteria (first meeting)

- [ ] They acknowledge **GA staffing or visibility** pain  
- [ ] Named **pilot owner** (dispatcher or vendor management)  
- [ ] Agreed **next step**: 30-min demo, NDA, or 5-ticket pilot scope  
- [ ] Enver (or equivalent) introduces **decision-maker** if not them  

## Metrics (track in spreadsheet or CRM)

- Calls placed / connected  
- Meetings booked  
- Pilots signed  
- WOs completed in pilot  
- Partner status calls per WO (target: &lt;1 per 5)
