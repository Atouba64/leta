# Positioning talk track — any partner call

Use on calls with Barrister, Qmatic-class OEMs, or regional MSPs.

## 60 seconds

> “I’m with **Leta** — we’re a Georgia-first platform for onsite IT fulfillment. Partners like you keep the customer relationship and the SLA; we provide **bench techs**, **live job status**, and **ticket-level messaging** so your team isn’t chasing ‘where’s the tech’ calls all day. We’re not trying to be another national broker — we’re the **modern layer** for markets where you need extra capacity or better visibility. We’d love a **small pilot** in Georgia: a handful of work orders, your partner ticket numbers, and clear metrics on fewer phone touches and on-time arrival.”

## 3 minutes (if they engage)

1. **Problem you’ve seen in the industry** — black-hole dispatch, Teams links, offshore bridges, rate fights on the phone.  
2. **What Leta built** — partner portal, tech marketplace with skills + distance filters, Leta Live voice on the ticket, digital close-out.  
3. **Who wins** — their dispatch desk (fewer calls), their customer (visibility), techs (fair accept flow), Leta (fee on completed WOs).  
4. **Ask** — 15-minute demo or 5-WO pilot in GA counties they name.

## Proof (honest — pilot stage)

- Live product: mobile app + partner portal path on [leta.repair](https://leta.repair/partners.html)  
- Founder **field-service background** (Barrister-class environment — speak from pattern, not gossip)  
- **1099 tech pipeline** via modern recruit (text/call + skills-based onboarding)  

## Close every call with

> “What would you need to see in a **5-ticket pilot** in Georgia for this to be worth routing our way?”
