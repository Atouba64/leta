# Outreach Strategy: Spencer Technologies

## 1. Value Proposition & Key Arguments
When speaking with Spencer Technologies, we must emphasize these core arguments:
* **Retail Specialization:** Our platform tags techs by skill (e.g., POS installations, digital signage). You get techs who actually know retail IT.
* **Audit-Ready Closeouts:** Spencer's biggest headache is techs leaving without getting the store manager's signature. Leta enforces signature and photo capture *before* the ticket can be closed.
* **Overflow Capacity:** Keep your W2s in major cities; use Leta for overflow and rural Georgia routes to reduce your travel expenses.

## 2. Target Personas
To break into Spencer Technologies, we need to target the following roles:
* VP of Field Operations
* Director of Vendor Relations
* Project Manager - Retail Deployments

## 3. Contact Avenues
* Partner onboarding application via their corporate website.
* LinkedIn targeting: "Retail Deployment Manager" or "Field Services Vendor Manager".
* Industry conferences (NRF, retail IT shows) are where they recruit partners.

## 4. The "Leta Difference" Pitch for Spencer Technologies
"You are experts at managing national and global IT portfolios. We are the experts at executing the physical layer in Georgia. By partnering with Leta, you bypass the friction of managing disparate 1099s and gain an invisible, highly accountable field force that makes your SLA compliance effortless."
