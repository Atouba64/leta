# Step-by-Step Execution Guide: Closing Spencer Technologies

*Reference `/07-partner-accounts/00-LETA-PARTNER-ACQUISITION-PLAYBOOK.md` for the Master Sales Playbook.*

1. **Research Phase:** Identify the Vendor Manager and CTO/VP of Tech Ops on LinkedIn.
2. **Technical Pitching (Days 1-5):** Send the Cold Email from `03-call-scripts-and-emails.md`. Ensure you mention the specific technical advantage (e.g., Offline-first apps, SAGA microservices, CRDT inventory syncing) that applies to Spencer Technologies.
3. **Pilot Securing (Days 6-10):** Ask to handle just 3 high-friction tickets in Georgia to prove the data integrity of the Leta platform.
4. **Integration:** Share our API documentation and establish the Bidirectional Gateway or Portal handoff.
