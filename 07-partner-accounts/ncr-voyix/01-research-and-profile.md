# Partner Profile: NCR Voyix

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Atlanta, GA
* **Website:** https://www.ncrvoyix.com
* **Core Focus:** Enterprise technology for retail, restaurants, and digital banking (POS systems, self-checkout).

## 2. Mode of Operation
Formerly part of NCR Corporation, Voyix is a titan in retail/restaurant IT. They have a massive global footprint. Being headquartered in Atlanta, they handle thousands of local deployments and break-fix tickets right in Leta's backyard. They use a hybrid of in-house customer engineers and third-party localized vendors.

## 3. How Leta Serves Them
Being geographically aligned with their HQ gives Leta a massive strategic advantage. We can act as their premier local fulfillment arm. When a self-checkout lane goes down in a Georgia grocery store, Leta dispatches a certified tech faster than their internal queue can manage.

## 4. Operational Alignment
* **Dispatch Model:** Leta integrates as a primary fulfillment arm for their Georgia-based tickets.
* **Platform Fit:** They can utilize the Leta Partner Portal to inject work orders, or Leta can consume tickets from their existing portal, keeping status updated in real-time.
