# Leta Tech: Platform and Channel Targets

To build a massive funnel of demand, Leta Tech must register across a wide spectrum of procurement systems. This casts a net over everything from quick break-fix tickets to massive enterprise deployments across Georgia.

## 1. Enterprise CMMS & Facility Management Platforms
Massive multi-site operators (retail, restaurants, convenience stores, grocery chains) use these platforms to route work orders directly to approved vendor networks.
* **ServiceChannel:** The holy grail for multi-site retail and hospitality. Major brands use this to route POS swaps to secure network outages directly to localized service companies.
* **Corrigo (by JLL):** The heavyweight for commercial real estate, corporate enterprise office parks, and healthcare facilities.
* **fmPilot (by CBRE / FacilitySource):** Massive footprint in big-box retail, grocery store chains, and large logistics warehouses.
* **Verisae (by Accruent):** Highly utilized by 24/7 operations like convenience stores, gas stations, and grocery networks.
* **Fexa:** A modern facility management platform favored by multi-site operators for direct-to-vendor routing.

## 2. Corporate Vendor Management Systems (VMS) & Procurement
Fortune 500 companies, massive banking networks, and enterprise logistics firms use corporate procurement software to manage regional deployments.
* **SAP Fieldglass:** The premier corporate VMS used by major corporations for large-scale IT infrastructure rollouts.
* **Beeline:** Heavily utilized by telecom networks, financial institutions, and healthcare systems.
* **Ariba Network (SAP):** One of the largest B2B procurement networks in the world. Often required simply to process invoices and purchase orders.
* **Coupa:** A massive corporate spend and procurement platform.
* **Magnit (formerly PRO Unlimited):** Used by enterprise organizations to source, manage, and track contingent regional service labor.

## 3. The Direct IT Field Service Marketplaces
The lowest barrier to entry for immediate work orders, often serving as overflow routing for national MSPs.
* **Field Nation:** The largest volume marketplace for IT, telecom, digital signage, and POS field services.
* **WorkMarket (by ADP):** Heavily used by enterprise IT aggregators to manage contingent labor routing.
* **CloudWork|PRO:** A modern competitor focused strictly on IT, POS, and networking with zero standard payout fees for technicians.
* **Tech-Connect:** Targeted IT field service management platform.

## 4. National Aggregators & Deployment Partners (MSPs/Integrators)
Global or national Managed Service Providers (MSPs) that win massive deployment contracts but require regional execution arms like Leta Tech.
* **Source Support Services:** Handles mission-critical OEM and enterprise infrastructure, dealing with data centers, healthcare systems, and industrial automation (HQ in Lawrenceville, GA).
* **TechLink Services:** Specializes in national network infrastructure, SD-WAN, smart IoT devices, digital signage, and smart lockers.
* **CompuCom:** Massive provider of end-to-end managed IT services, hardware lifecycle solutions, and digital workplace consulting with a massive footprint in Atlanta.
* **Telaid Industries:** Technology deployment partner executing physical installations of complex systems (CCTV, access control, electrical systems).
* **Kinettix:** Focuses on global IT field service coordination, providing staff augmentation and deployment oversight.
* **Spencer Technologies:** Massive player in retail IT deployments, frequently partnering for high-volume POS and digital signage installations.
* **Essintial Enterprise Solutions:** Provides enterprise technology support requiring a robust network of local service companies to fulfill SLAs.

## 5. Educational Infrastructure
* **State University Systems (USG):** Major universities (UGA, Georgia Tech, Kennesaw State) require maintenance of smart classrooms, campus Wi-Fi, digital signage, and server rooms.
* **Direct K-12 District Contracts:** County school boards issuing local RFPs for massive device rollouts (Chromebooks/iPads) and smartboards.

## 6. Federal Subcontracting
* **Department of Defense (DoD) Military Bases:** Bases like Fort Moore, Fort Eisenhower, and Robins AFB rely on defense contractors (General Dynamics, SAIC) who need local "smart hands".
* **Veterans Affairs (VA) Medical Centers & Clinics:** IT needs ranging from patient check-in kiosks to secure telehealth infrastructure. Target prime vendors on sam.gov.
* **GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) - IT Category:** Partner with thousands of companies holding GSA schedules who need local technicians for federal IT deployments.

## 7. State and Local Civic Infrastructure
* **State-Level Master IT Contracts (Team Georgia Marketplace/DOAS):** Register Leta Tech here to bid on state, county, or local municipal IT contracts.
* **Public Housing Authorities (HUD):** Local housing authorities managing residential complexes needing CCTV, access control, and low-voltage cabling.
* **Regional Airport Authorities & The FAA:** Airports require constant maintenance of digital flight info displays, security checkpoints, and POS systems.
* **Sourcewell:** A cooperative purchasing network allowing public sector agencies to bypass RFP processes. Partner with prime vendors holding Sourcewell contracts.

## 8. Smart Locker and Asset Management
* **Luxer One & Parcel Pending:** High-quality, cloud-connected package locker systems for residential, corporate, and university campuses.
* **Modern Office Systems:** Smart locker integration for financial institutions and complex building logistics.
* **Elite Storage Products:** National, turn-key locker rollouts for Fortune 500 companies.

## 9. Commercial Telematics and Fleet GPS Integration
* **Samsara:** Global leader in connected operations requiring certified installation partners for telematics, AI dash cams, and ELDs.
* **Radius Telematics:** GPS tracking software and OEM telematics requiring physical hardware setups and ongoing support.
* **Verizon Connect & GPS Insight:** Compliance management software, smart cameras, and asset monitoring for commercial fleets.

## 10. Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure
* **Qmerit:** Network of certified EV charger installers, acting as logistical conduit for automakers and EV charger manufacturers.
* **Blink Charging:** Global public charging network involved in major dealership infrastructure programs.
* **Regional Georgia Integrators:** Firms like Integrity EV Solutions, Ampt Electric, and Capital City Electrical Services that can use Leta's electricians as variable-cost sub-contractors.
