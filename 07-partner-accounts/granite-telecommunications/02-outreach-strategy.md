# Outreach Strategy: Granite Telecommunications

## 1. Value Proposition & Key Arguments
When speaking with Granite Telecommunications, we must emphasize these core arguments:
* **Telecom & IT Fusion:** Leta can supply techs who bridge the gap between pulling cable and configuring SD-WAN routers.
* **Unmatched Transparency:** Granite built its reputation on customer service. Leta's timeline-based tracking allows Granite to pass real-time updates directly to their end clients, maintaining their premium service reputation.

## 2. Target Personas
To break into Granite Telecommunications, we need to target the following roles:
* Director of Partner Networks
* Vendor Manager
* Service Delivery Director

## 3. Contact Avenues
* Granite "Premier Partner" portal application (very structured process).
* Networking with Granite Regional Channel Managers.
* LinkedIn outreach to Granite Vendor Management.

## 4. The "Leta Difference" Pitch for Granite Telecommunications
"You are experts at managing national and global IT portfolios. We are the experts at executing the physical layer in Georgia. By partnering with Leta, you bypass the friction of managing disparate 1099s and gain an invisible, highly accountable field force that makes your SLA compliance effortless."
