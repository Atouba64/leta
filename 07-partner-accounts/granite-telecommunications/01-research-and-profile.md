# Partner Profile: Granite Telecommunications

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Quincy, MA
* **Website:** https://www.granitenet.com
* **Core Focus:** Telecom solutions, networking, and IT infrastructure for multi-location businesses.

## 2. Mode of Operation
Granite aggregates telecom lines but also provides heavy IT and network installation services. They have a massive "Premier Partner" program utilizing thousands of local cabling and IT contractors to service retail and commercial clients nationwide. They are very demanding on SLAs.

## 3. How Leta Serves Them
Leta can become a Granite Premier Partner. Because Granite is heavily focused on telecommunications (demarc extensions, router installs, SD-WAN turn-ups), Leta can provide highly specialized networking techs across Georgia.

## 4. Operational Alignment
* **Dispatch Model:** Leta integrates as a primary fulfillment arm for their Georgia-based tickets.
* **Platform Fit:** They can utilize the Leta Partner Portal to inject work orders, or Leta can consume tickets from their existing portal, keeping status updated in real-time.
