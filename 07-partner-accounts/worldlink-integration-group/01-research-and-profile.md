# Partner Profile: Worldlink Integration Group

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Lake Forest, CA
* **Website:** https://www.worldlinkintegration.com
* **Core Focus:** National IT deployment, integration, and field services.

## 2. Mode of Operation
Worldlink is a pure-play deployment and integration company. They specialize in staging hardware and sending it out for national rollouts. They explicitly market themselves as utilizing a "vast network of vetted field technicians." They are exactly the type of company that needs a regional super-aggregator like Leta.

## 3. How Leta Serves Them
Instead of Worldlink having to vet 15 different small IT shops in Georgia to handle a statewide rollout, they can assign the entire Georgia scope to Leta. We handle the local logistics and execution.

## 4. Operational Alignment
* **Dispatch Model:** Leta integrates as a primary fulfillment arm for their Georgia-based tickets.
* **Platform Fit:** They can utilize the Leta Partner Portal to inject work orders, or Leta can consume tickets from their existing portal, keeping status updated in real-time.
