# Partner Profile: Pomeroy

## 1. Company Overview
* **Headquarters:** Hebron, KY
* **Website:** https://www.pomeroy.com
* **Core Focus:** IT infrastructure edge, workplace services, and network solutions.

## 2. Mode of Operation
Pomeroy provides end-to-end IT infrastructure support. Similar to CompuCom, they manage massive enterprise environments (hospitals, corporate campuses). They utilize a robust partner network to extend their reach into areas where maintaining W2s is not cost-effective.

## 3. How Leta Serves Them
Leta provides Pomeroy with geographic coverage in secondary and tertiary Georgia markets (e.g., Augusta, Columbus, Savannah) where they may lack dense W2 coverage, ensuring they meet their SLAs for state-wide clients.

## 4. Operational Alignment
* **Dispatch Model:** Leta integrates as a primary fulfillment arm for their Georgia-based tickets.
* **Platform Fit:** They can utilize the Leta Partner Portal to inject work orders, or Leta can consume tickets from their existing portal, keeping status updated in real-time.
