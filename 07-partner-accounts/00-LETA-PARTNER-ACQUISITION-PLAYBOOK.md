# LETA PARTNER ACQUISITION: THE MASTER PLAYBOOK

## Introduction
This playbook is the definitive, self-explanatory guide to closing enterprise IT partners for Leta. If a new sales representative or operations manager joins Leta tomorrow, reading this document will give them 100% of the context they need to start selling our services.

---

## 1. Who Are Our Partners?
Leta is a B2B (Business-to-Business) fulfillment engine. We provide "Smart Hands" (physical IT technicians) in the state of Georgia. Our partners are companies that have clients in Georgia but lack the physical, local W2 workforce to service them cost-effectively. 

We target two distinct types of partners. You must understand the difference to pitch them correctly:

### Type A: National Service Providers (NSPs) / Aggregators
* **Examples:** Kinettix, Spencer Technologies, Bailiwick.
* **Their Business:** They win massive national contracts (e.g., "Install new Wi-Fi in 2,000 Target stores nationwide").
* **Their Problem:** They cannot hire W2 employees in all 50 states. They rely heavily on independent 1099 contractors, which is a logistical nightmare to manage (techs showing up late, ghosting, failing to take photos).
* **The Leta Pitch:** "Stop managing 50 different flaky 1099s in Georgia. Give Leta your entire Georgia scope. We guarantee the SLA, and our software forces the techs to upload photos and signatures before they leave."

### Type B: Managed Service Providers (MSPs)
* **Examples:** Leapfrog IT Services, Integris.
* **Their Business:** They manage the day-to-day IT for medium-to-large businesses. They have highly paid, brilliant engineers sitting in a central office (Network Operations Center / NOC).
* **Their Problem:** When a client's router breaks in a branch office 3 hours away, the MSP loses money paying their senior engineer to sit in traffic just to plug in a cable.
* **The Leta Pitch:** "Keep your Tier 3 engineers remote and billable. Use Leta's 'Smart Hands' for the physical dispatch. Your remote engineer can talk directly to our on-site tech through the Leta Portal."

## 2. Required Arsenal (Have These Ready)
Before you call these companies, you must have your corporate paperwork ready. Enterprise partners will NOT do business with us without these three things:

1. **W-9 Form:** Proves Leta is a registered US business for tax purposes.
2. **COI (Certificate of Insurance):** You must have General Liability and Worker's Compensation insurance (usually $1M to $2M minimum coverage). They will ask for a COI with their company listed as an "Additional Insured."
3. **MSA (Master Service Agreement):** Most of these partners will send *you* their MSA to sign. It dictates payment terms (often Net-30 or Net-45, meaning we get paid 30-45 days after the ticket is closed).

## 3. The Leta Differentiators (Why We Win)
When a partner asks, *"Why should I use Leta instead of FieldNation or WorkMarket?"*
* **FieldNation is the Wild West:** "On FieldNation, you post a job and pray a good tech picks it up. You have to vet them yourself. Leta is a managed vendor. We take accountability for the ticket. If our tech fails, it's on us, not you."
* **Audit-Ready Compliance:** "Our mobile app natively prevents a technician from closing a ticket without uploading the required deliverables (site photos, manager sign-off). You never have to chase us for paperwork."
* **Anchor Coverage:** "We don't just cover Atlanta. We cover the interstates down to Macon, Valdosta, and Savannah. We are your true Georgia backstop."
