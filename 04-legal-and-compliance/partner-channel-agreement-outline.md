# Partner channel agreement — outline (not legal advice)

Engage counsel before use. Covers **fulfillment partner** contracting Leta for Georgia (or broader) onsite IT.

## 1. Parties & roles

- **Partner:** Tier 2 (MSP, OEM, FSN) with end-customer or upstream contract
- **Leta:** Platform + field bench coordinator
- **Field tech:** 1099 via Leta (not Partner employee)

## 2. Scope of services

- Onsite break/fix, install, smart hands per ticket
- Optional remote overwatch (Leta Live)
- Geography: defined MSAs/counties

## 3. Ordering & performance

- Orders via portal/API/email template
- SLA tiers attached as exhibit
- Partner provides accurate POC and access data
- **GUS:** Partner warrants authority to request onsite access

## 4. Rates & payment

- Rate card exhibit; change control process
- Mobilization, minimums, after-hours
- Invoice terms (net-30 typical)
- Dispute window + evidence (timeline, photos)

## 5. Independent contractor chain

- Leta not Partner’s employer
- Techs not Partner’s employees
- Partner shall not solicit techs off-platform for Partner-originated work (term + carve-outs)

## 6. Insurance & indemnity

- Mutual GL requirements
- Partner indemnifies Leta for site access disputes arising from Partner bad data
- Leta indemnifies for tech negligence within scope

## 7. Data protection

- DPA exhibit if PII exchanged
- Retention & deletion for photos/logs

## 8. Confidentiality & branding

- White-label comms optional
- No misrepresentation of employment

## 9. Term & termination

- Pilot term; convert to annual
- Wind-down: complete open tickets
- Survival: payment, indemnity, confidentiality

## 10. Limitation of liability

- Cap = fees paid in prior 12 months (counsel to set)
