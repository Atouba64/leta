# Field technician 1099 agreements — Leta (outline)

**Important:** This file is an **internal outline** for topics to cover with a **licensed attorney**. It is **not** legal advice and not a template ready for signature.

## Objectives

- Clarify **independent contractor** relationship (vs employee) under governing law.  
- Set expectations for **quality**, **SLA adherence**, **professional conduct**, and **brand representation** (white-label vs co-branded).  
- Protect Leta and partners with **confidentiality**, **data use**, and **non-solicitation** provisions as appropriate.

## Sections typically required (checklist)

1. **Parties and effective date**  
2. **Scope of services** — categories of work the tech may perform via Leta  
3. **Contractor representations** — licenses, insurance, tools, vehicle  
4. **Compensation** — payout schedule, adjustments, chargebacks, tips policy (if any)  
5. **Expenses** — who covers mileage, parking, parts float  
6. **IP and work product** — customer deliverables, photo rights, platform IP  
7. **Confidentiality** — customer, partner, and Leta data  
8. **Non-solicitation** — especially partner clients in B2B flows  
9. **Safety and PPE** — site access rules  
10. **Termination** — for cause / convenience, appeal window  
11. **Dispute resolution** — arbitration vs courts (jurisdiction-specific)  
12. **Insurance requirements** — align to [`insurance-requirements.md`](./insurance-requirements.md)  
13. **Indemnity** — allocate risk for negligence and third-party claims  
14. **Classification backup** — behavioral guidelines consistent with contractor status

## Operational hooks

- In-app **policy acknowledgements** on rule changes.  
- **Versioned** agreements stored with acceptance timestamps.
