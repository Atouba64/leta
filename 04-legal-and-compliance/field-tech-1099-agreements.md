# Field technician 1099 independent contractor agreement (outline)

> **Not legal advice.** Engage Georgia-licensed counsel before execution.

## Overview

Relationship between **Leta** and independent field technicians using the platform to source work orders.

## 1. Independent contractor status

- **Classification:** 1099 independent contractor—not employee, agent, or partner.  
- **Autonomy:** Right to accept or decline any work order; Leta does not dictate hours.  
- **Equipment:** Tech provides standard toolkit, transportation, smartphone capable of Leta app + **Leta Live** WebRTC.

## 2. Platform usage & dispatch rules

- **Auto-accept window:** High-rated techs may receive priority notifications; accept = binding commitment to that ticket's SLA.  
- **No sub-delegation:** Accepting tech must be the individual on site; unvetted substitution → removal.

## 3. Professional conduct & white-label

- On partner-sourced jobs (e.g. Qmatic), tech represents partner seamlessly.  
- **Non-solicitation:** No poaching end-customer contacts on site.  
- Use **Request Remote Expert** for technical blockers—not improper burden on local POC beyond physical access.

## 4. Payment terms

- Rates **locked at acceptance**.  
- Release after POC sign-off + required photos.  
- **Target: payout within 24 business hours** after clearance.
