# Leta core differentiators (the "Leta Advantage")

Leta wins by building a platform that **natively understands the reality of the field**—not by out-spending legacy brokers on ads.

## 1. Native "Remote Expert" video routing ("Leta Live")

No more emailing Microsoft Teams links. The Leta field app includes **native WebRTC video**. When a tech is at a rack and needs help, they press **Request Remote Expert**. The app connects them to Leta overwatch or the client's tier-3 support, **auto-linking the session to the ticket**.

## 2. Automated dispatch & auto-accept

Eliminate the passive phone-call bottleneck. Techs receive **algorithmic push notifications** based on live geolocation, skill set, and ratings. High-tier techs get a **5-minute head start** to auto-accept premium jobs before they hit the general board.

## 3. The "Access & POC" blueprint

Every Leta ticket includes a standardized **On-Site Friction** tab: POC name/photo, rack unlock codes, and physical access instructions (e.g. "Roof access via back stockroom").

## 4. Transparent multi-tier contracting ("GUS")

When Spectrum hires Qmatic, and Qmatic hires Leta, **Qmatic engineers** can securely view the Leta tech's live status and video feed—without Barrister-style phone operators in the middle.
