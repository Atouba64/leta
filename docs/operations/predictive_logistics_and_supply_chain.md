# Predictive logistics and supply chain

The primary catalyst for ~50% job cancellation at legacy providers is the chasm between **field dispatch** and **parts logistics**. Deploying the best technician is meaningless if the logic board never arrived.

## Leta integration model

Leta integrates:

- Enterprise client inventory systems
- Regional **Forward Stocking Locations (FSLs)**

Machine learning over failure rates, seasonality, and environment predicts **localized shortages before they occur**.

## Workflow

1. High-probability failure detected → automatic **kitting request** at nearest FSL.
2. Auto-dispatch routes tech to **retrieve pre-staged, tested equipment** before client site.
3. On-site arrival implies **parts + skills** alignment → higher first-time fix.

## Outcomes

- Eliminates "waiting on parts" paradigm
- Reduces secondary trips and fuel waste
- Direct counter to Essintial-style arrive-without-hardware failures
