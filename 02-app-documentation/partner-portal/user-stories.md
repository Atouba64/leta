# Partner portal / partner dashboard — user stories (Leta)

Audience: **fulfillment partners** (e.g. national/regional dispatch organizations and similar service companies) that route **their** work orders through Leta for **Georgia** field execution. This complements direct customer demand in the same marketplace.

## Epic: Visibility and confidence

- **As a partner dispatcher**, I want to see **which tickets are matched, en route, on-site, or blocked** so that I can answer my client’s “where’s the tech?” question with facts.  
- **As a partner ops lead**, I want **GPS-informed status** (within policy) so that I can manage SLAs without calling the tech repeatedly.  
- **As a partner account manager**, I want **photos, checklists, and signatures** attached to the ticket so that I can prove delivery to my customer.

## Epic: Fulfillment and coverage

- **As a partner**, I want to publish work orders that **auto-notify** qualified techs within a **configurable mile radius** so that rural and suburban jobs get coverage, not just downtown cores.  
- **As a partner**, I want **fallback rules** (widen radius, raise payout, escalate internally) when no tech accepts within a time window.

## Epic: Economics and reconciliation

- **As a partner finance user**, I want **per-ticket economics** (payout, platform fee, adjustments) exported or API-synced so that reconciliation matches our contract.  
- **As a partner**, I want **rate cards and SLAs** enforced in software so billing surprises are minimized.

## Epic: Relationship and brand

- **As a partner**, I want **white-label or co-branded** customer comms (per contract) so the end client experience matches our agreement.  
- **As a partner**, I want clarity that Leta is a **capacity partner**, not a scheme to disintermediate my client relationship—reflected in **non-solicitation** and data-handling terms (legal drafts in [`../../04-legal-and-compliance/`](../../04-legal-and-compliance/)).

## Non-goals (initial)

- Replacing the partner’s full PSA/CRM—**integrate** or export; do not require migration day one.
