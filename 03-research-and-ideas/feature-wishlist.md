# Feature wishlist — Leta

Backlog ideas captured from product vision. **Prioritize** ruthlessly; not everything belongs in MVP.

## Customer experience

- [ ] **Live GPS-style tracking** with clear lifecycle states  
- [ ] **Upfront estimate** with rate card transparency  
- [ ] **In-app chat** tied to ticket + optional masked phone bridge  
- [ ] **Multi-site / multi-device** cart for B2B  
- [ ] **Procurement** modes: PO, cost center, approval workflows

## Field technician

- [ ] **Active** toggle + smart push offers by radius  
- [ ] **Offline mode** for checklist + deferred media upload  
- [ ] **Tooling checklist** and van inventory hints  
- [ ] **Navigation** deep links  
- [ ] **Instant / same-day payout** options (subject to risk controls)

## Remote overwatch

- [ ] **Escalate** → **WebRTC** session with minimal taps  
- [ ] **Screen share** policies for supported devices  
- [ ] **AI-assisted** runbook suggestions (later; privacy reviewed)

## Partner / B2B

- [ ] **Hybrid dashboard:** map, photos, signatures for partner ops  
- [ ] **Webhook + API** for PSA integration  
- [ ] **White-label** comms templates  
- [ ] **SLA breach** alerting and auto-escalation to partner dispatchers

## Trust, safety, fraud

- [ ] **ID verification** ladder for techs  
- [ ] **Device attestation** for high-risk jobs  
- [ ] **Chargeback** defense packet generator (evidence bundle)

## Analytics

- [ ] Partner scorecards: **FTFR**, CSAT, margin per ticket  
- [ ] Heatmaps for **underserved** zip codes

## Nice-to-have / later

- AR-assisted cable tracing (speculative)  
- Parts marketplace integration  
- Multi-state licensing tracker for expansion
