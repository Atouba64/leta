Georgia first

IT service on demand—with the field bench to actually show up.

Leta connects companies that already sell IT field service (and need more onsite capacity) with a Georgia-wide network of professional technicians—through one transparent platform. Direct customers can also request a tech the same way they would order a ride or a meal: clear status, clear outcomes.

View project on GitHub Partner or pilot inquiry

For partners (dispatch & field service companies)

If you already have clients and work orders but struggle to staff every Georgia market with qualified onsite techs, Leta is built as a fulfillment layer—not a competitor to your relationships. Examples of the kind of organizations we mean include national and regional networks such as Barrister Group LLC and similar service providers.

Coverage

Geo-aware matching so tickets inside a reasonable mile radius are more likely to be accepted and completed on time.

Transparency

Partner-facing visibility into status, proof of work, and structured close-out—so your ops team spends less time chasing updates.

Growth

Take on more work from your existing book of business without linear W-2 hiring in every zip code.

For technicians

Leta aggregates demand from partner-originated tickets and direct customer orders into one pool—so when you are Active, you see more relevant offers and less idle time. The goal is steady, defensible work in Georgia, with modern tooling and fast payouts (per product policy).

  • Push-based offers matched to your skills, radius, and rating.
  • Escalation path to remote experts when a job gets complex on-site.
  • Digital close-out: signatures, photos, and artifacts that protect you and the customer.

For customers (businesses & individuals)

Order onsite IT help with the same clarity you expect from on-demand logistics: who is coming, when, and what it will take to complete the job—backed by professional workflows, not a black box.
