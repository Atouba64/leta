We help you fulfill onsite IT—not compete for your accounts.

Leta is a centralized platform for the lifecycle of professional IT field service in Georgia: partner and customer demand, dispatch, onsite execution, remote escalation when needed, and payout. If you are a fulfillment partner with real work orders and tight SLAs, we are built as capacity + visibility alongside your brand—not a parallel sales force going after your book of business.

What we are

  • A fulfillment layer when you already won the ticket but need qualified bodies in a Georgia radius.
  • A product mindset: status, routing, evidence—the kind of surface engineering and ops teams expect.
  • Co-strengthening with national and regional field networks: you bring demand and trust; we help deliver.

What we are not

  • Not here to disintermediate your enterprise contracts or rebrand your customer relationships by default.
  • Not a generic “gig” noise platform—scope is professional IT workflows, SLAs, and close-out discipline.
  • Not pretending nationwide hyper-scale on day one—we are deliberate about Georgia depth first for quality.

For fulfillment partners

Dispatch companies, MSPs, and Barrister-class national or regional IT service organizations: if your constraint is onsite headcount in every metro and corridor, Leta matches work to vetted field professionals with geo-aware rules—so your ops team spends less time chasing status and more time hitting commitments.

Demand stays yours

Partner-originated work orders remain your customer story in the partner-led model. Leta’s job is delivery mechanics and transparency—not channel warfare.

Liquidity for techs

We also aggregate direct customer orders into the same technician pool. That improves utilization, which helps your tickets get picked up when the market is thin.

Built like infra people think

One platform, clear states, audit-friendly artifacts—closer to how you’d design an internal service than a consumer toy. Georgia-first rollout is intentional QA before geography expands.

For customers

Businesses and individuals who need onsite IT get ordering and tracking with on-demand clarity: who is coming, when, and what “done” looks like—without living in voicemail loops.

  • B2B & B2C — branch IT, break/fix, installs, and similar professional scopes as the network matures.
  • Transparency — status you can trust; professional close-out, not a black box.
  • Fair economics — marketplace efficiency and less rework as liquidity builds (pricing follows published policy).

Mobile apps are the operational product; this page is for trust and routing. App store links land here when builds are public.

For field technicians

1099 professionals in Georgia: combined partner + direct demand in one Active pool—more relevant offers, modern tooling, faster payouts per policy, and escalation to remote experts when a job gets hard on-site.

  • Offers matched to skills, radius, and quality signals.
  • Digital close-out: signatures, photos, and artifacts that protect you and the customer.
  • “Overwatch” path—remote experts on live video/audio when you need backup.
