# Partner playbook (operator)

Sales + ops quick index. Full workflows: [`../../06-partner-operations/`](../../06-partner-operations/).

## Pitch in one minute

“You won the SLA; we staff Georgia with vetted techs, live map/timeline, and close-out packages — you keep the customer story.”

## Pilot offer

- 5–10 work orders
- One region + one remote-heavy ticket type
- Weekly CSV billing

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “We already use Barrister / Field Nation” | Coopetition — we fill GA gaps + visibility layer |
| “Techs will go off-platform” | Contract + pay only through Leta on your WOs |
| “Too new” | Pilot with cancel-anytime after open tickets close |
