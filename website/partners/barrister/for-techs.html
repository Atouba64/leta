Leta Tech app

Run Barrister tickets like a Barrister tech — on Leta

When you work a partner job, you are still a Leta contractor. The app shows the partner work order number, site POC, and dispatch voice — optimized for phone-heavy dispatch, not retail consumer tickets.

In the app

  1. 1

    Dispatch board

    Toggle Partner dispatch (Barrister) to filter. Offers show WO #, POC name, and POC-only warnings.

  2. 2

    Offer detail

    Read scope, tap to call POC, or use Call dispatch on Leta so your personal number stays private.

  3. 3

    Active job

    Confirm WO still open, navigate to site, message thread, proof photos, signature — same audit trail Barrister expects.

Non‑negotiables on partner sites

POC only

Do not call the store main line unless dispatch approves. The named POC is on the ticket.

Confirm WO before you roll

Tap Confirm WO still open after speaking with dispatch — prevents no-pay drive-bys.
