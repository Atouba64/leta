Modern tech bench

The right tech for the ticket — not one-size-fits-all

Leta closes the gap between your dispatch desk and skilled field techs the way modern marketplaces do: we recruit and onboard with the same rigor as top freelancing platforms — social, text, and a deep mobile application — then let techs run their own profiles. You get transparency; they get control.

Cost-effective coverage

Need smart hands at a sensible rate? Route to motivated generalists and side-hustle techs building hours — ideal for high-volume break-fix and standard rollouts.

Specialists when it matters

Cradlepoint, POS, networking, low-voltage? Tag the work order with required skills and Leta surfaces techs who broadcast those abilities on their profiles — the same way you’d pick a freelancer for a niche project.

Distance-aware matching

Techs filter and sort offers by miles, pay, and skills — so you are not paying for wild drives, and they only accept jobs they can win. Less churn, fewer no-shows.

Unlike legacy field networks where everyone looks the same on paper, Leta’s marketplace shows you headline, skills, proficiency, and travel radius before you commit — so you choose economy or expertise per ticket.
