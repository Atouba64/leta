# Short-form video scripts — Leta Tech recruit

Low production, phone-shot, local GA energy. Post to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. Target metros in `recruitMetros` in contact-config.

---

## 1. Day in the life (POV) — 30–45s

| Beat | Visual | VO / on-screen text |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| 0–3s | Alarm, grab phone | “POV: you’re on the Leta Tech crew” |
| 3–8s | Open Leta app, tap Active | “You decide when you work” |
| 8–15s | Accept offer, map to site | “Offer pops · you take it or pass” |
| 15–22s | On-site, simple fix (cable, router) | “Real field IT · not a call center” |
| 22–28s | Close-out in app (photo tap) | “Tap done · get paid” |
| 28–35s | Friends, food, sunset | “Still got your evening” |
| CTA | Logo + text | “Link in bio · Georgia only · WhatsApp or apply” |

**Caption:** Your phone. Your schedule. Join Leta Tech in [city]. #sidehustle #fieldtech #atlanta

## Ad targeting notes

- **Geo:** 25 mi radius around Atlanta, Athens, Augusta, Columbus, Macon, Savannah.
- **Age:** 18–26 (broaden to 28 if CPM is high).
- **Interests:** side hustle, gig worker, IT, gaming, campus life (where available).
