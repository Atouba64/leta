# Short-form video scripts — Leta Tech recruit

Phone-shot, vertical, captions burned in. Post to TikTok, Reels, Shorts. **Rotate geo** weekly (see [social-media-strategy.md](./social-media-strategy.md)).

---

## 1. Day in the life (POV) — 30–45s · Metro / campus

| Beat | Visual | VO / on-screen text |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| 0–3s | Alarm, grab phone | “POV: you’re on the Leta Tech crew” |
| 3–8s | Open app, tap Active | “You decide when you work” |
| 8–15s | Accept offer, maps | “Offer near you · take it or pass” |
| 15–22s | On-site fix | “Real field IT · not a call center” |
| 22–28s | Close-out in app | “Tap done · get paid” |
| 28–35s | Evening free | “Still got your night” |
| CTA | Logo | “Link in bio · Georgia · text or apply” |

**Caption:** Your phone. Your schedule. Join Leta Tech in [city]. #sidehustle #fieldtech #georgia
