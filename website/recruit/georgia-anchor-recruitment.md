## One post template (Facebook / IG)

> **1099 field tech (IT) · [ANCHOR] area — Georgia statewide**  
> Leta dispatches break-fix and retail IT near you — not only Atlanta.  
> Smartphone + car · you pick offers by miles and pay.  
> Text **[phone]** or apply: **leta.repair/technicians.html#join**

Replace `[ANCHOR]` with: Macon, Savannah, Dalton, etc.

## Hyper-local ad clusters ($5–15/day)

Rotate **one anchor per week** — never ATL-only spend.

| Week | Geo target | Creative hook |
|------|------------|---------------|
| 1 | Macon + Warner Robins | “Center of GA — short drives, real IT tickets” |
| 2 | Savannah + Brunswick | “Coast jobs without ATL traffic” |
| 3 | Valdosta + Tifton | “I-75 South GA logistics IT” |
| 4 | Gainesville + Athens | “North GA — mountains + college towns” |

## Macon-specific (mandatory narrative)

Macon is the **geographic center** (I-75 × I-16). Every statewide recruit campaign should include at least one Macon creative:

- *“Middle of the state = middle of the money — if you live near Macon, you see more offers per mile than anywhere else in GA.”*
