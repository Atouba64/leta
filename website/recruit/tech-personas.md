# Leta Tech — who we recruit (Georgia statewide)

Leta runs on **1099 field technicians** spread across Georgia. Partners and customers only win if the bench is deep in **every corner of the state** — not just Atlanta. These personas guide copy, ads, and onboarding questions.

## Primary personas (design for all four)

### 1. Campus & side-hustle hustler
- **Age:** 18–26 · **Where:** Atlanta, Athens, Statesboro, Kennesaw, Clarksville, Savannah colleges
- **Day job:** Student, retail, delivery, gaming lounge staff
- **Why Leta:** Flexible blocks between classes; “tech” brand on résumé; cash faster than campus jobs
- **Reach:** TikTok/Reels, Instagram, campus flyers, Discord, barber shops near campus
- **Message hook:** “Your phone. Your schedule. Not a desk job.”

### 2. Gig & field veteran
- **Age:** 22–40 · **Where:** Metro Atlanta suburbs, Macon, Columbus (Fort Moore spillover), Augusta
- **Background:** Field Nation, Geek Squad agent, cable/ISP install, AV hang-and-bang, break-fix contractor
- **Why Leta:** Better close-out story, overwatch on hard tickets, Georgia-only focus (less dead drives)
- **Reach:** Facebook gig groups, Reddit r/Atlanta jobs threads, Telegram channels, word of mouth
- **Message hook:** “Fewer wild drives. Jobs that match your skills.”

### 3. Rural & small-town builder
- **Age:** 21–45 · **Where:** South GA, North GA mountains, Valdosta, Albany, Dalton, Rome, Brunswick
- **Background:** Family IT help, church/school volunteer tech, former military comms, auto shop with computer side work
- **Why Leta:** Paid travel radius; supplemental income without relocating; respected as “the tech person” locally
- **Reach:** Facebook community groups (county-level), local radio sponsorships, church bulletins (QR), barber shops
- **Message hook:** “Serve your county — we dispatch by radius, not just Atlanta.”

### 4. Career switcher & underemployed pro
- **Age:** 25–50 · **Where:** Anywhere with vehicle + smartphone
- **Background:** Help desk, retail management, warehouse, trades helper learning IT
- **Why Leta:** Low barrier vs. bootcamp debt; prove skills on real tickets before cert spend
- **Reach:** Indeed/Facebook “contract” posts (low budget), library workforce boards, TikTok “day in the life”
- **Message hook:** “Hustle and curiosity beat a degree on day one.”

## What they all must have (non-negotiable)

| Requirement | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Smartphone + mobile data | Offers, maps, close-out |
| Reliable vehicle | Georgia is car-first outside MARTA |
| Legal work authorization | 1099 on customer sites |
| Willingness to complete web onboarding | Skills, availability, background consent |
| Professional on-site behavior | Brand = trust for partners |

## What we do **not** require on day one

- College degree or CompTIA (certs help routing, not gatekeeping)
- Atlanta residence
- Full-time availability

## Copy tone by persona

| Persona | Words to use | Avoid |
|---------|--------------|-------|
| Hustler | crew, vibe, your hours, tap in | corporate, applicant, duties |
| Veteran | offers, radius, close-out, payout | startup jargon overload |
| Rural | your county, your community, drive radius | “metro only” |
| Switcher | learn on real jobs, prove it | “senior engineer” |

## Onboarding data we need (web form)

Captured on [tech-onboarding.html](../tech-onboarding.html) — do not duplicate in app except email match:

- Legal name, contact, birth year, city/ZIP (maps them to region)
- Vehicle, license, smartphone
- Home metro / travel radius
- Skills checkboxes + experience + motivation essays
- 1099 + background check consent

## Referral flywheel

Enthusiastic techs know other enthusiasts — especially hustlers and rural builders with tight local networks. Pilot: bonus after N completed jobs (see `contact-config.js`).
