# Master Vendor Registration Packet

To get approved on major enterprise CMMS (ServiceChannel, Corrigo), VMS platforms (SAP Fieldglass, Beeline), and government portals (Team Georgia Marketplace), Leta Tech cannot simply create an account with an email address. These entities require rigorous corporate vetting.

This document serves as the internal checklist and repository structure for our standardized "Vendor Packet" to rapidly streamline onboarding across all aggregators, MSPs, and procurement networks.

## Core Documentation Required

1.  **Legal Business Entity Documents**
    *   Articles of Organization / Incorporation (State of Georgia)
    *   State Business License / Certificate of Good Standing
    *   Employer Identification Number (EIN) Issuance Letter from IRS
    *   Signed W-9 (Current Year)
    *   Dun & Bradstreet Number (D-U-N-S) - Highly recommended for federal and enterprise contracts

2.  **Insurance Capabilities (Certificate of Insurance - COI)**
    *   **General Liability:** Typically $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate.
    *   **Workers' Compensation:** Statutory limits as required by Georgia, plus Employer's Liability.
    *   **Commercial Auto Liability:** Required for field technicians utilizing vehicles for dispatch.
    *   **Errors and Omissions (E&O) / Cyber Liability:** Essential for IT field service, data center environments, and handling POS/Network data. Often requires a $1M to $5M limit depending on the VMS.
    *   *Note: Most platforms will require adding them as an "Additional Insured" upon contract execution.*

3.  **Financial & Payment Infrastructure**
    *   Standard Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) / ACH Authorization Form
    *   Voided Company Check or Bank Verification Letter
    *   Standard Invoice Template referencing specific PO numbers and Work Order IDs
    *   Accounts Receivable (A/R) Contact Information

4.  **Operational & Capabilities Profile**
    *   **Capability Statement:** A one-page executive summary detailing Leta Tech's core competencies (POS Rollouts, Telematics, Edge Deployments, Smart Lockers, EV Infrastructure), geographic coverage (Georgia Statewide), and API dispatch capabilities.
    *   **List of NAICS and NIGP Codes:** Essential for State/Federal registration (e.g., 541512 Computer Systems Design Services, 541519 Other Computer Related Services, 238210 Electrical Contractors).
    *   **Diversity Certifications:** If applicable (MBE, WBE, VOSB), these drastically increase subcontracting appeal.

5.  **Security & Compliance Framework (The "Trust Center")**
    *   **Vendor Code of Conduct & Ethics Acknowledgement:** Pre-signed generic adherence statement.
    *   **Data Processing Agreement (DPA) / Security Posture Statement:** Documentation detailing how Leta Tech’s AI routing and offline-first architecture isolates and protects enterprise client data (mitigating "Black Box" AI risks).
    *   **Background Check Policy:** Formal documentation of Leta Tech’s technician vetting process (e.g., standard background check, motor vehicle records, CJIS/Public Trust clearances for Tier 3 gov work).
    *   **Drug-Free Workplace Certification**

## Strategy for Execution

1.  **Compile the Repository:** Store clean PDF versions of all these documents in a secure, accessible cloud drive (e.g., Google Drive / Dropbox / Box).
2.  **Create a Dedicated Vendor Email:** `vendor-compliance@leta.repair` to handle all CMMS/VMS automated communications and portal login links.
3.  **Rapid Deployment:** When engaging a new MSP (e.g., CompuCom) or CMMS (e.g., ServiceChannel), Leta Tech will submit the entire compiled packet immediately, signaling operational maturity and expediting activation from weeks to days.
