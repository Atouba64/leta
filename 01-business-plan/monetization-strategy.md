# Monetization Strategy & Revenue Model

Leta operates on an asset-light, platform-as-a-service (PaaS) model. Because our technicians are 1099 Independent Contractors, our overhead remains strictly tied to software maintenance, marketing, and core internal operations.

## 1. The Core Revenue Engine: Transaction Take-Rate

Every time a work order is successfully completed and paid for through the Leta app, the platform retains a percentage of the total transaction.

- **Customer Price:** The total price quoted to the B2B or B2C customer (calculated by estimated time, urgency, and skill level required).
- **Tech Payout:** The pre-agreed rate paid to the Field Tech (and Remote Tech, if utilized).
- **Leta Margin (The Take-Rate):** Leta retains a 20% to 30% margin on every ticket for facilitating the connection, providing the software infrastructure, handling payment processing, and offering insurance/dispute resolution.

## 2. B2B Enterprise Subscriptions (SaaS)

For larger companies in Georgia (e.g., a regional retail chain with 50 locations), paying per-ticket can be unpredictable.

- **Leta Pro (Monthly Retainer):** Businesses pay a monthly subscription fee (e.g., $299/month).
- **Benefits Included:** Guaranteed 2-hour dispatch SLAs (Service Level Agreements), discounted hourly rates on actual repairs, dedicated account managers, and detailed monthly IT health analytics.

## 3. Remote Tech Consulting Fees

When a Field Tech requires escalation, they ping a Remote Tech.

- If a customer specifically requests a highly specialized Remote Tech (e.g., a Cisco Certified Network Expert) to guide an entry-level Field Tech, Leta charges a premium "Expert Assist" surcharge, split between the platform and the Remote Tech.

## Payout Structure (The Techs)

To attract the best talent away from legacy competitors, Leta promises **Fast, Frictionless Payments**.

- Funds are held in escrow via integration with Stripe.
- Once the customer signs off on the completed job via the Field Tech's app, funds are released.
- Techs can choose standard direct deposit (2-3 days) or pay a small 1% fee for "Leta Instant Payout" directly to their debit card.
