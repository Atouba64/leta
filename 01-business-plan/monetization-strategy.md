# Monetization strategy — Leta

How Leta captures value while staying predictable for customers and fair for technicians.

## Primary revenue streams

### 1. Take-rate on completed work (core)

- Leta clips a **percentage** or **flat platform fee** per completed ticket.  
- Aligns incentives: revenue when jobs finish cleanly and quickly.  
- Pair with transparent **rate cards** so customers see **estimated cost before dispatch** (reduces disputes).

### 2. B2B partner / enterprise tiers (subscription + usage)

| Tier (concept) | Who | What they pay for |
|----------------|-----|-------------------|
| **Starter** | Small MSPs / regional IT shops | Per-seat portal access, basic reporting, standard dispatch SLAs |
| **Growth** | Multi-city partners | API access, branded customer comms option, priority routing |
| **Enterprise** | Large service orgs | Custom SLA packages, dedicated success, advanced analytics |

Subscription can be **per dispatcher seat**, **per managed location**, or **minimum monthly platform fee** with overage on ticket volume.

### 3. Value-add services (optional, later)

- **Financing / factoring** partnerships for faster tech payouts (careful regulation).  
- **Insurance** bundles or verified coverage upsells.  
- **Premium remote overwatch** minutes (usage-based).

## Gross margin guardrails (example: ~30% target)

A headline **30% gross margin** is a reasonable **starting hypothesis** only if **internal costs** are modeled explicitly. Watch these buckets:

| Cost driver | Why it matters |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Insurance** | General liability and **E&O** protect against costly mistakes on client equipment. |
| **Travel / truck roll** | Clarify whether travel time and mileage are **in the tech payout**, **billed to partner**, or a **separate line item**. |
| **Payment processing** | Cards, ACH, instant payouts—each has fees. |
| **Taxes & compliance** | 1099 processing, potential future W-2 hybrid roles, sales tax on software fees (jurisdiction-dependent). |
| **Support & dispute ops** | Chargebacks, rework, and QA staffing erode margin if SLAs are loose. |

## Pricing mechanics that reduce friction

- **Rate cards** for common SKUs (e.g. printer repair, PC imaging, network drop) instead of “open-ended hourly” only.  
- **Surge / distance** modifiers published up front (geo-aware dispatch).  
- **Cancellation windows** with clear customer and tech policies.

## Partner economics (white-label vs branded)

- **Subcontractor / white-label:** partner may pay **higher take-rate** for Leta to stay invisible—value is pure fulfillment.  
- **Strategic partner / co-branded:** Leta may accept **lower take-rate** if the brand accrues to Leta and drives direct demand.

Document the chosen model per contract (see [`../04-legal-and-compliance/customer-SLA.md`](../04-legal-and-compliance/customer-SLA.md) and [`operational-model.md`](./operational-model.md)).
