# Georgia rollout strategy — business plan excerpt

**Data source:** [`../data/georgia-coverage.json`](../data/georgia-coverage.json)

## Strategic choice

Leta wins by being **excellent in one state** before being mediocre in ten. Georgia is large enough to support a standalone marketplace; small enough to build **anchor density** competitors skip.

## Geographic thesis

1. **Metro Atlanta** — revenue proof, partner pilots, recruit flywheel.  
2. **Macon / Warner Robins** — geographic center; highest **miles of coverage per tech** in the state.  
3. **Corner hubs** — Savannah, Augusta, Columbus, Gainesville, Athens — so enterprise RFPs cannot disqualify you for “ATL only.”  
4. **Corridor cities** — Tifton, Valdosta, Dalton, Rome, LaGrange, Newnan, Statesboro — connect hubs along I-75, I-16, I-85.  
5. **SW deep corner** — Bainbridge / Albany — last mile for true statewide box.

## Phased investment (capital-efficient)

| Phase | Timeline (indicative) | Spend focus | Success metric |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|----------------|
| 0 | Months 0–6 | ATL recruit + product | 40+ active techs ATL; 1 partner pilot |
| 1 | Months 6–12 | Hub recruit (6 anchors) | Each hub ≥ `targetTechsMin` |
| 2 | Months 12–18 | Corridor recruit + FB geo ads | <2 hr median dispatch to corridor WO |
| 3 | Months 18–24 | SW corner + rural long-radius | Partner-signed “statewide GA” statement |

## Revenue mix (Georgia-only)

| Stream | Notes |
|--------|-------|

| Direct SMB / retail | Atlanta + secondary metros |

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Overclaim coverage | Phase-gated marketing; public rollout section on coverage.html |
| Rural no-shows | Radius + payout gates; WO confirm before roll |
| Single-anchor dependency on ATL | Macon hub narrative in every partner deck |
