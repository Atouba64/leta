# Remote Technical Support Specialist: The Playbook

## Your Role at Leta
As a Remote Technical Support Specialist, you are the "Air Traffic Controller" and "Level 2 Brain" for our field technicians. You sit between the Partner (e.g., NCR, Kinettix) and the Field Tech on site. 

Your goals are:
1. Ensure the Field Tech succeeds on site.
2. Ensure the Partner gets perfect, audit-ready deliverables.
3. Protect the Leta SLA.

## 1. Daily Operations & The AI Agent
You have **Level 3 Access** to the Leta AI Agent. 
* If a tech is stuck on site (e.g., "The Cradlepoint router isn't getting an LTE signal"), you can query the AI Agent to instantly pull the troubleshooting steps from the `08-tech-ops-partner-guidelines` database.
* You are responsible for reviewing the photos the tech uploads before clicking "Release Tech from Site."

## 2. Escalation Matrix
If a tech encounters an unresolvable issue on-site:
* **Step 1:** The Tech messages you in the Leta Live Thread.
* **Step 2:** You query the Leta AI Agent for known fixes or documentation.
* **Step 3:** You guide the tech through the fix.
* **Step 4:** If it requires a replacement part, YOU (the Remote Specialist) contact the Partner's dispatch center to request an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization). You do not make the field tech sit on hold.

## 3. Tech Quality Assurance (QA)
You act as the QA layer. 
* Did the tech follow the white-labeling rules?
* Are the photos blurry? (If yes, reject the deliverable and make the tech retake them before leaving).
* Was the tech professional in the thread?
