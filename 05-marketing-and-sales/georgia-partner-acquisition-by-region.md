# Partner acquisition — Georgia by anchor

Use this when a prospect says *“Do you cover [city]?”* Pull `partnerPitch` from [`../data/georgia-coverage.json`](../data/georgia-coverage.json).

## Universal opener

> Leta is the modern fulfillment layer for onsite IT in **Georgia** — you keep the customer and WO number; we add **bench depth at eighteen anchor markets**, live ticket status, and phone-first field execution that migrates to your portal over time.

## Outreach sequencing (low budget)

1. **Warm intro** in one anchor city (founder network).  
2. Pilot **5–10 WOs** tied to that anchor’s geography.  
3. Case study → adjacent anchor on same corridor (e.g. Macon → Tifton → Valdosta).  
4. Only then claim **corridor-complete** on that interstate.

## Objection: “You’re just Atlanta”

Response: Open [coverage.html](https://leta.repair/coverage.html) — walk through **Macon as geographic center** and corner anchors. Offer pilot WO in *their* city with KPI on status calls per ticket.
