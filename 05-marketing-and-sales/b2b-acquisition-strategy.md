# B2B client acquisition strategy

> Competitor context: [`../03-research-and-ideas/competitor-analysis.md`](../03-research-and-ideas/competitor-analysis.md)

## Overview

Leta does **not** market consumer laptop repair. Targets: **Tier 2 MSPs**, **OEMs** (e.g. Qmatic), and **enterprise IT** needing localized smart hands in **Georgia**.

## Priority channel: fulfillment partners ("Barrister-class")

Highest-leverage partners: established dispatch / field IT orgs with large client databases and steady work-order flow but chronic **onsite tech shortages** in Georgia. See [`partner-channel-win-win.md`](./partner-channel-win-win.md).

## 1. Security-first sales motion

Legacy brokers treat deployments as manual labor. Leta treats edge work as **cybersecurity operations**.

- **Pitch:** "We deploy with zero-trust access controls and auditable field execution—not just truck rolls."  
- **Authority:** Leadership technical credentials (Azure, Security+, CySA+, Pentest+) signal engineer-operators, not call-center dispatch.

## 2. Direct outreach to regional MSPs

- **Target:** Mid-size MSPs—retail chains, medical, government contractors in Atlanta / North Georgia.  
- **Tactic:** Executive demos of partner portal vs legacy "black hole" visibility; live GUS communication failure narrative.

## 3. Pilot program offer

- **Offer:** "Five low-priority tickets in Gainesville/Atlanta radius—track live on Leta. If we are not faster and clearer than your incumbent, labor is free for that pilot set."  
- **Goal:** Replace Barrister-class vendors on proof, not slogans.
