# B2B acquisition strategy — Leta

How Leta wins **anchor partners** and scales demand without losing the Georgia-quality story.

## Ideal first partners

- **Regional MSPs** with after-hours overflow and inconsistent coverage in outer metros.  
- **OEM-adjacent** service brokers needing **proof-of-work** artifacts.  
- **Property / retail chains** with distributed small-footprint IT (optional vertical).

## Value proposition by partner type

| Partner pain | Leta response |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Labor bottleneck** | Surge capacity with **SLA-backed** dispatch |
| **No visibility** | **Hybrid dashboard**—map, photos, signatures |
| **Quality risk** | QA program + **overwatch** for complex jobs |
| **Brand risk** | White-label Option A vs co-brand Option B (see [`../01-business-plan/operational-model.md`](../01-business-plan/operational-model.md)) |

## Acquisition channels

1. **Direct outbound** to MSP owners in Atlanta + secondary cities  
2. **Channel referrals** from distributors / VAR communities  
3. **Case studies** after pilot metrics (FTFR, ETA accuracy, CSAT)  
4. **Partner conferences** / local tech council sponsorships (budgeted)

## Pilot packaging

- **90-day pilot** with limited SKUs and explicit rate card  
- Weekly business review: SLA hits/misses, margin per ticket, tech feedback

## Pricing conversation

- Lead with **predictability** (rate card + portal transparency), not lowest price.  
- Show how **30% gross margin** targets survive insurance and processing when modeled honestly.
