# Georgia coverage — one-pager (sales & partners)

**Live map:** https://leta.repair/coverage.html

## Headline

**Leta covers Georgia with eighteen anchor markets — not just Atlanta.**

## The map in one breath

- **Metro Atlanta** — density and SLA proof.  
- **Macon / Warner Robins** — geographic center (I-75 × I-16); fastest reach to middle-of-state work.  
- **Corners:** Dalton (NW), Bainbridge/Albany (SW), Savannah/Brunswick (SE), Augusta (E), Columbus (W).  
- **Corridors:** Valdosta & Tifton (I-75 south), Gainesville (I-985), Athens & Rome (north), LaGrange/Newnan (I-85 west), Statesboro (inland east).
