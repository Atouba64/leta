# Partner channel positioning — the "anti-legacy" pitch

## Overview

When speaking to partners, address pain with legacy networks and position Leta as the modern fulfillment layer.

## 1. Why change? (legacy problems)

| Pain | Legacy pattern | Leta answer |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Black hole** | Ticket sent; no visibility until someone answers 1-800 | Live map + timeline in partner portal |
| **Communication breakdown** | Email Teams links; offshore hold queues | **Leta Live** WebRTC tied to ticket |
| **Bench gap** | Cannot staff every GA radius | Geo-aware pool + partner + direct liquidity |
| **Channel fear** | Broker competes for end customer | Partner keeps narrative; Leta is fulfillment layer |

## 2. Win-win value proposition

**For partner (OEM/MSP):** GPS arrival, status timeline, Tier 3 joins field camera instantly, SLA protection, PSA sync.

**For end customer:** Prepared tech, proper channels, secure close-out.

**For Leta techs:** Fair rates, 24h payouts, no platform tax story—see [`tech-onboarding-funnel.md`](./tech-onboarding-funnel.md).

## 3. Coopetition framing

Barrister-class firms may be **competitors on paper** and **partners in practice** when bottleneck is fulfillment capacity—see [`../03-research-and-ideas/competitor-analysis.md`](../03-research-and-ideas/competitor-analysis.md).
