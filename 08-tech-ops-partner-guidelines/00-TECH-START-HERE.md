# Field Technician Partner Guidelines

## Welcome to the Leta Field Force
As a Leta Technician, you don't just represent Leta—you represent our national enterprise partners. This folder contains the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for each specific partner. 

**Before you drive to any site, you MUST read the SOP for the partner assigned to your ticket.**

### What you will find in each partner's folder:
1. **White-Label Rules:** Who do you say you work for when you walk in the door?
2. **Dress Code:** Do they require a polo, boots, or specific PPE (Personal Protective Equipment)?
3. **On-Site Deliverables:** What photos and signatures MUST be uploaded before you can leave?
4. **Checkout Procedures:** Who do you call to get released from the site?

*Note to AI Agents: This directory is tagged for [Access Level: Field Tech].*
