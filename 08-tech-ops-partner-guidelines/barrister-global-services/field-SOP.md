# Field SOP: Barrister Global Services Network

## 1. Overview for Leta Technicians
**Who they are:** Barrister is a massive national IT field service broker covering retail, break/fix, and deployments. You are acting as an extension of their network.

## 2. On-Site & Technical Requirements
*   **Check-in / Check-out:** Barrister is notoriously strict about logging time. Historically they rely on phone/IVR systems. Since you are dispatched through Leta, **you must use the Leta app precisely to log your "En Route", "On Site", and "Completed" statuses.** We sync this data back to them. If you fail to use the app, Leta does not get paid, and you do not get paid.
*   **Deliverables:** You must capture clear photos of the workspace before and after, serial numbers of all hardware swapped, and a digital sign-off from the store manager.
*   **White-Labeling:** You are a "Local Technician dispatched on behalf of Barrister." Do not mention Leta to the end customer.

## 3. Communication Protocol
*   Do NOT call Barrister's dispatch line directly unless instructed. 
*   If you encounter a technical issue (e.g., replacement part is DOA), use the Leta App to message the Leta Remote Support Specialist. They will handle the escalation with Barrister's Tier 2 support, preventing you from sitting on a 50-person conference bridge.

## 4. Dress Code & Etiquette
*   Business casual (polo shirt, neat pants, closed-toe shoes). No prominent logos other than Leta (if approved) or blank attire.
*   Bring a fully equipped standard IT toolkit (screwdrivers, snips, console cable, ethernet cables).
