# Leta Tech Technician Tiers

Structuring the technician database into clear, well-defined tiers allows us to match the right skill level to the right job (protecting margins and reputation) and creates a built-in career progression path that keeps technicians motivated to upskill.

Here is the framework for Leta Tech's three-tiered technician profiles, designed to be used during onboarding, background checks, and the credential verification process.

## Tier 1: Leta Tech Junior Technician (Entry-Level)

This is the "boots on the ground" tier. They make up the bulk of the workforce and represent raw potential. They act as smart hands under the guidance of remote support or Leta Tech's internal training protocols.

**Qualifications:**
* Zero prior IT experience required.
* Must pass a standard background check.
* Possess a valid driver's license and have reliable transportation.
* Demonstrate a strong willingness to learn and follow detailed instructions.

**Scope of Work:**
* Basic hardware deployments and physical swap-outs (e.g., replacing a pre-configured router or plugging in a new monitor).
* Site surveys and taking photos of equipment racks for remote engineers.
* Assisting Mid-Level or Senior Techs on large-scale rollouts (e.g., pulling cables, unboxing hardware, trash removal).

**Clearance & Perks:**
* Access to entry-level field dispatch tickets.
* Direct on-the-job training and mentorship to prepare them for their CompTIA A+ certification.

## Tier 2: Leta Tech Field Technician (Mid-Level)

These are reliable, independent problem solvers. They have proven their baseline knowledge and can be trusted to execute standard IT deployments without constant hand-holding.

**Qualifications:**
* Verified baseline certifications (e.g., CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, or Cisco CCNA).
* Must pass the background check.
* Proven track record (either through promotion from Tier 1 or verified past employment).

**Scope of Work:**
* Point of Sale (POS) installations and troubleshooting.
* Network rack patching, switch configurations, and router installations.
* Workstation troubleshooting, OS imaging, and peripheral diagnostics.
* Leading small deployment teams of Tier 1 Junior Techs.

**Clearance & Perks:**
* Unlocked access to a significantly broader and more lucrative pool of work orders.
* Higher hourly or flat-rate payouts.
* Priority routing for regional field dispatches.

## Tier 3: Leta Tech Senior Specialist / Engineer (Advanced-Level)

This is the elite tier. These individuals handle complex, high-stakes environments and specialized disciplines. They are the premium talent Leta Tech offers to enterprise clients.

**Qualifications:**
* Top-tier, highly specialized certifications (e.g., CISSP, OSCP, AWS Certified Solutions Architect, CCNP) or demonstrable senior-level proficiency in specific programming languages (Rust, Ruby, Python).
* Thorough background and deep credential verification required.

**Scope of Work:**
* Penetration testing and advanced cybersecurity auditing.
* High-level enterprise architecture, cloud migrations, and remote infrastructure management.
* Custom script creation, automation engineering, and deep network diagnostics.

**Clearance & Perks:**
* Highest compensation bracket.
* Granted the autonomy to choose their projects.
* Full access to Leta Tech's premium work orders, including the option to work 100% remotely for consulting, coding, and remote-support contracts.

## Leta Tech Technician Tier Comparison

| Feature | Tier 1: Junior Tech | Tier 2: Field Tech | Tier 3: Senior Specialist |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Experience Needed** | None | Certified / Verified | Highly Specialized |
| **Primary Work Type** | Physical labor, simple swaps | Installations, networking, POS | Security, architecture, coding |
| **Work Environment** | 100% On-Site | Mostly On-Site | On-Site or Remote |
| **Job Access Level** | Restricted / Entry | Broad / General | Unrestricted / Premium |
| **Compensation** | Base Rate | Elevated Rate | Premium / Negotiated Rate |
