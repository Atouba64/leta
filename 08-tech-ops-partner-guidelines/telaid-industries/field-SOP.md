# Field SOP: Telaid Industries

## 1. Overview for Leta Technicians
**Who they are:** Telaid is an established technology deployment partner that executes physical installations of complex systems like CCTV, access control, smart lockers, and electrical systems.

## 2. On-Site & Technical Requirements
Physical installations (CCTV, Lockers, Low Voltage). Bring standard PPE (hard hat, safety glasses, high-vis vest) to all Telaid jobs. If you are delayed, the Leta AI-dispatch engine will automatically detect this and may re-route another tech. Communication in the app is critical.

## 3. Leta Technology Integration
**How we process this partner:**
Telaid's deployments often take place in high-friction environments with strict compliance. Leta’s AI-dispatch engine dynamically allocates computing resources entirely to geospatial routing and exception management. If an unexpected emergency blocks a Telaid installation, Leta instantly recalculates the regional board and re-routes the closest qualified personnel without waiting for a human dispatcher.

*Note: Because our technology specifically solves their backend problems, you MUST use the Leta App precisely as trained so the data syncs correctly to Telaid Industries.*

## 4. White-Label & Professionalism
* Represent Telaid Industries to the end-client. 
* Do not discuss Leta dispatch rates, smart contracts, or AI routing with the client.
